I am obsessed with this range. Almost every turn of the ride today was photo worthy. The terrain was gracious enough to take it easy on me today and give me plenty of time for photo stops. I think today was my favorite day of the trip so far.

An early morning 20 mile blast took me past a deep canyon carved by the Lewis River. Then I suddenly crossed over the Snake River. I was surprised to see it again as I passed over it back in Idaho, I guess the Snake snakes.

Then the Tetons started peaking out. I’ll let the photos do the rest of the talking.

As I turned east and faced away from the Tetons, I was able to watch them shrink through my beer view mirror. While heading away, it felt like the mountains were pushing me along and encouraging me onwards, or maybe it was just a tailwind.

We linked up with another eastbound biker. Hung Ming from Taiwan. He’s taking the long way across the country, hauling a larger load than I would have thought possible, and getting by with next to no English. Looking forward to piece together some more of his stories over the next few days.
