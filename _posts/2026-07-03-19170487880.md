Hot and windy day out here in Kansas, I mean eastern Colorado. Today we biked through 2 towns, each of which had nothing to offer for weary bike travelers. We were able to sneak some hot water out of an unlocked fire department sink. Lunch consisted of a wide spread of sweet and savory snacks as the restaurant we were planning to stop at was closed for the holiday. Dad did great on his first big day with all the gear!

We broke the day up into 10 mile sections, trying to find shade where possible. The terrain was mostly rolling hills and cross winds.

The most interesting things we passed on the road today were some unidentifiable sites. The first being a large scale construction site of unknown industry. They had a 5 mile long water pipe with mobile pump stations providing water to the site, over 50 semi trucks hauling strange funnel shaped devices to and from the site, and a 40 foot tall wind barrier constructed along 2 sides of the site (40.6233054, -103.9861942). The second was were I took an emergency bathroom break in an unlocked porta potty. This toilet was next to a heavily secured pad of concrete, with cameras, prison like fences, and antennas. It appeared to be the entrance to some underground facility/storage. Missile silo? (40.6120604, -103.7622086)

We’re spending the night in a cute motel in Sterling, CO. Booking hotels sucks. I’m stuck in a catch 22 where we don’t have a reservation number for a room we booked but can’t get through the AI help desk to a real human without a confirmation number. My brain feels like it’s going to explode.

Had a great burger and beer while watching the Argentina vs Capo Verde game. Finished the night off with a scoop of root beer ice cream while watching a storm roll in.
