Today’s ride consisted of a wide variety of terrain. Smooth roads, protected bike paths, and surprisingly cute towns were the highlights. Deep sand, loose gravel, and ice busted Michigan pavement were the lowlights. I hate to be dramatic, but today may have been the bumpiest day of the trip so far. I’m thankful to be running such a light weight setup for this section, as I would be worried about bags falling off my bike under these conditions.

The small town of Marshall caught our eye today. A beautiful marble fountain welcomed us to town and the farmers market off Main Street made it feel homey. Downtown was full of cute shops and restaurants, and the river had a nice bike path and was flowing fast enough to tube along it. Passing through Marshall prompted a conversation between my mom and I about whether or not we could imagine living in a small town like this. In a lot of ways, we both agreed we could.

We continued the brewery tour today, stopping for lunch at Malleable Brewing Company in Albion, then a cold drink at Grand River Brewery in Jackson. I can’t quite tell if I’m just getting really picky with my food or if I’m getting tired of dinning out. Regardless, I think some home cooking in Rochester will do a lot of good for my health and morale.

After arriving in Jackson, we pedaled around checking out art sculptures along the bike path and large murals (referred to as Bright Walls) on the buildings downtown. After making our way along another treacherous section of Michigan roadway, we stopped for ice cream at Frosty Boy.

The lack of rest days and the addition of a few daily beers has made my body start to ache and tire. No amount of sleep feels like enough and I find myself yawning throughout the day. I think I may need an entire day dedicated to sleeping.
