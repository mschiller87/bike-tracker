Wonderful amazing day today. Sunny blue sky, cool lake breeze, quiet coastal roads, lovely new company. Couldn’t have asked for more on this unsuspecting gem of a day. The weather forecast showed rain for the whole week. Recent budget cuts/de-funding bills have left the national weather service severely understaffed and lacking the money to launch as many weather balloons. I’ve gotten to witness the extreme inaccuracies of these forecasts throughout my trip. The forecast now feels more similar to the forecasts we made in elementary school; walking outside and announcing the weather in real time.

We slept in and had a relaxing late start. An early lunch of Chipotle and smoothies, enjoyed with lake front views, provided fuel for another 20 miles. I made a mandatory stop at the first Sheetz convenience store of the trip. It did not meet the same level of excellence that Casey’s exhibited. I felt like I needed to be much hungrier or very high to enjoy it to its fullest potential. 

Along the ride, we rolled up on a lemon aid stand right when our thirst levels required something sweet. The kids gave us a great 3 for $1 deal, it was a cute experience.

Our final destination was Geneva on the Lake. I knew nothing about this town before arriving. It was like a fantasy land for a 10 year old kid from the 80’s. Arcades, amusement parks, themed restaurants, and gift shops galore. We spent the better part of the evening at Adventure Land where we played putt putt golf, raced go karts, and hit some balls in the batting cages. I ate two separate rounds of meals at Eddie’s Grille, getting a cheese burger on both occasions.

We caught sunset on a grassy knoll overlooking the water. It was hard to remember that we were still in Ohio.
