Today felt like a fairy tail. If you’ve never been to Lake Michigan, I don’t know if I’ll be able to describe it to you, but I’ll try my best. The vibe is vacation every day, the air is fresh, the water is tropical, the scenery is sandy with tree lined shores, the dress code ranges from beach formal to west coast casual. You can find homes right on the coast with incredible porches cantilevered over the water, and right down the block you can find a log cabin surrounded by maples and oaks. It feels like home in every town you stop in.

The ride today took longer than normal because I found myself stopping every quarter mile to take pictures. I’m fascinated by unique architecture tucked into beautiful landscapes. I’m sure I showed up on a few Ring doorbells with my camera in hand causing some confusion for the homeowners.

I stopped at the last Casey’s on my route. It’s a sad day but I was able to cash out all of my rewards points to get myself a free Gatorade. I could always count on Casey’s to deliver tasty food, clean bathrooms, and cold water. Hopefully a new gas station chain surfaces to sustain me for the remaining 1000 miles.

My final destination for the day was South Haven, MI. This has been on my radar since the beginning of the trip and has been a huge motivating factor and milestone achievement for me. After entering my home state, I queued Radical Face’s, “Welcome Home, Son” and got pretty emotional. The weight of the trip so far became very clear to me and I found myself in disbelief that I’ve made it this far. I was welcomed in by my parents and our good friends Dave and Laura. I got the full South Haven experience with a dingy ride on the lake, sandbar football, and waterfront beers.

Tomorrow, my mom and I set out on the stretch across Michigan!
