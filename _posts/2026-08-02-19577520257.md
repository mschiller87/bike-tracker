Despite my deep, icy sleep last night, I still found myself yawning all day. A sizable breakfast burrito in the morning got the day started on a good note. After eating, I spent the rest of the morning lounging on Nick’s couch as he packed up his apartment in preparation for a big move. I got some good reading in and mapped out the remaining days of my trip. Believe it or not, I’m going to be in New York City by August 12th!!! I’m trying my best to not get tunnel vision and focus too hard on that end date. I’m certain that once I get to the city, life is going to start moving way faster than any speeds I’ve hit on my bike. I want to savor the last 10 days of this trip as much as possible.

Luckily, my friends Dom and Austin are going to help me with that! When left to my own devices, I tend to bike unreasonable distances. However, when more reasonable people join me on a ride, I am naturally corralled into a conversational pace with more breaks and photo opportunities. They will be joining me for the next two days!

As you may have noticed, today’s entry is lacking in the photo department. It’s not from a lack of effort. Despite having the whole day to do this short distance, I chose the one single hour of continuous rain to ride in. Not wanting to get my camera wet, I resorted to some iPhone photography, but even this provided some uninspiring results. I hate to say it, but the parts of Cleveland I saw today were not very photogenic, especially in the overcast rain. I made a promise to myself to reserve more time and energy to put back into photography, as this was one of my goals for the trip. So stay tuned for improved photos.
