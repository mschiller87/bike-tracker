Believe it or not, it was hot again. I guess the whole country is hot right now (except Santa Cruz), so I’m sure you understand what I’m feeling. We got up early today and hit the road by 6am, which actually felt like 5am since we entered central time zone yesterday. This made a world of a difference, starting before the sun ever rose, gave us a huge head start on the heat. It wasn’t until around noon when temps climbed over 90 that the heat started to affect our performance.

I stopped at the first Casey’s gas station of the trip. This place is an A tier roadside food stop and was serving up loaded breakfast burritos this morning. I will be ordering their cheeseburger pizza next time I pass by one. A footlong subway sandwich served as a nice snack and carried me the last 15 miles to Oxford, NE.

We have developed quite an efficient method for locating a place to sleep. Locals usually spot our bikes and get curious, we tell them about our trip and explain our worries about camping outside in the heat, they suggest names and places of people/establishments who may be able to help us out. Today, we met Chloe at the gas station while eating some ice cream cookie sandwiches. She connected us with Pastor Brian at the local Baptist Church, who immediately drove down to meet us and was thrilled to open his doors to us for the night. The church provided an air conditioned gymnasium all for us. We parked our bikes, unloaded our gear, then walked one block to the local pool where we got to experience scenes straight out of the movie Caddy Shack while cooling off in the pool. It’s hard to describe the feeling of arriving in these small towns, living amongst the locals, and being welcomed in like one of their own. It was what I was hoping to experience when I set out on this trip, and it fills me with optimism seeing it come to fruition.

As if Oxford hadn’t already done enough for the crazy three bikers rolling through town, Chloe’s mom arrived at the church with a home cooked dinner for us to chow. Salad, goulash, garlic bread, brownies, and snacks for tomorrow. I feel like a movie star, but probably even better. 

Strawberry Super Stuffed: 6.5/10 (tastes exactly like the regular strawberry but probably ruined the regular version for me because I will always be looking for more filling).
