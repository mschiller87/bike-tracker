Wyoming is the best. We had a huge pass to climb today but the views were so beautiful, I hardly felt like I was working that hard.

I was the last one out of camp this morning at 7:30am. Everyone else in the group was intimidated by Togwotee Pass (TOE-guh-tee), a 3,000 foot 15 mile climb. The whole way up, I had hazy views of the Tetons behind me. At to top of the pass, I reached my new highest elevation of the trip, 9,858 feet.

It was here that I met Bernie/Sea Otter. He has been walking all over the world for the past 15 years. He is currently 80 days into his trip from Washington DC to the pacific coast in Washington State. He was so high spirited, he would have probably talked with me for hours if I didn’t have to keep moving. Happy Trails Sea Otter!

The road followed the Wind River on the descent from Togwotee. It brought me past the most amazing views of Pinnacles Buttes and Lava Mountain. The rocky peaks gave way to rolling hills with the incredibly swirly Wind River winding through it all. Suddenly the green and blues turned to dusty reds and oranges as we neared Dubois. It felt like scenes from southern Utah compete with cowboys herding cattle atop desert mesas.

Dubois is a cute little town. I took my first shower in 4 days at the laundromat. The entrance to the building was a huge buffalo skull, neat. We spent the night in a church, I’m thanking God for the protection from the mosquitos tonight.
