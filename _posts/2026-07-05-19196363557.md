Greetings Nebraska, it’s nice to finally meet you. I was a bit disappointed to see the shoulder on the road disappear immediately upon crossing into your state boundary, but you’ve got a couple hundred more miles to make up for it.

Today’s ride wasn’t anything new. More farms, more silos, more manure. We only passed through one town, Imperial. Here we got double cheeseburgers and milkshakes for lunch, and bagged 2 king sized burritos to saved for dinner. After lunch, the temperature had risen quite a bit and the wind started blowing against us. It’s crazy how fast you can get drained in heat like this. We pushed through the last 10 miles to get to Enders, NE.

The reservoir at camp was our saving grace. The water was colder than I expected and felt better than AC. We recouped at the beach for a bit, took a break to watch Norway vs Brazil, went back in the water, took a break to house our burritos, then watched the England vs Mexico game. The beach was packed full of people with boats and RVs backed right up the to water. The picture makes it look more overwhelming than it was.

The mosquitos came back with a vengeance after a few days in dryer climates. They devoured us both through socks, pants, and rain jackets. I’m glad the last soccer game of the night didn’t go to extra time. Going to bed itchy.
