I did it:)

Today was amazing. The riding felt effortless and like I could go on forever. I saw more shades of green than I even knew existed. I finally got to hug Clarabel.

It took a little longer than normal for me to get out of bed this morning. After a couple pep talks I finally fell back into my routine and got out on the road a little after 6. I stopped earlier than normal for breakfast since I wasn’t going to pass any more services for a while. I got my first official New York bacon egg and cheese on an everything bagel, it was delicious.

Most of the morning was spent climbing a gradual hill. This paid dividends in the afternoon when the rest of my ride was downhill. For lunch I stopped at Softie Boy and got 2 Nathan’s hotdogs🌭🌭, French fries, lemon-aid, and a soft serve (it was softie boy after all). This meal was the power up I needed and my average speed cranked up to 17mph. I kept thinking I would start to get glimpses of the city skyline as I neared the city but the forest was too dense and blocked any far views. The bike path wove through city parks, skirted along riverbanks, and ducked under overpasses. Until suddenly, the forest ended. All of the sudden, my 50 shades of green was replaced by a thundering subway above a bustling road with people on every mode of transportation under the sun trying to navigate an intersection. All of my confidence in my biking ability left with the greenery and I felt overwhelmed with the sensory overload. Luckily, a local man on his bike was also heading into the city and I was able to follow his lead to build my confidence back. I eventually got back to a protected bike path along the Hudson that led me straight to Clarabel. She met me at the little red lighthouse under the George Washington bridge. It was an emotional reunion and one we’ve both been eagerly awaiting.

We biked the rest of the way into the city together. Before hauling my bike inside her building, I wanted to get a little victory lap in around Central Park. I plugged in my headphones and blasted some house music while absolutely flying around the loop. It was the first time all trip that I pushed myself to the limit and gave it all the gas I could. I was clocking over 25mph passing ebikers, and at times throwing on the brakes while passing cops as the speed limit was only 15mph. I’m so excited to have this park in my backyard now and can’t wait to explore it further and break my own speed records.

With only one afternoon in the city so far, I’ve already had some amazing food. Pizza from Mama’s Too, and a Bahn Mi from Saiguette. We enjoyed our sandwiches lying in the grass in Central Park watching the firefly’s emerge from the darkness. Tonight I will go to bed smiling.
