I finally made it back to my hometown!

This whole idea of biking across the country started as a plan to bike from my home in Santa Cruz to my childhood home in Rochester. To have finally made it to my original destination feels like an incredible milestone. The extension to NYC came after Clarabel got into school in the city and we decided to move our lives out east. From here it’s just another 914 miles, piece of cake.

We took our time this morning after enjoying some of Dana’s homemade banana bread. The closer we got to Rochester, the more I started to recognize road names and scenery. I was glad to have my mom with me to point out places along the route that I wouldn’t have remembered on my own.

We stopped for brunch with Patti near Sylvan Lake and I got a massive breakfast sandwich on freshly baked focaccia. After another 15 miles, we stopped for lunch downtown Rochester at the world famous Lipuma’s Coney Island🌭. I am aware that my hot dog count is much lower than previously anticipated. I wish I could reassure you that this low turnout is only temporary but it turns out that hot dogs just don’t sit that well while biking in the heat. I will continue to pump the numbers up as best I can.

The route home from Lipuma’s is one I’ve traveled many times before. My GPS was not necessary for this stretch, although surprisingly, the hill along the way seemed to have grown since I last remember.

Getting home was a relief. It felt so good to clean up and flop into my bed in my very own bedroom. I relished this feeling for a few hours before jumping back into motion. All of the gear I had sent home over the course of the trip needed organized, I had to take a drug test for my new job, and we had family coming over for dinner soon.

I’m looking forward to some rest days at home, but may try to work in some short rides to keep my legs fresh. Starting Friday, I’ll begin my final push to the Big Apple. This section will consist of some stout solo days along some beautiful trails. 

Thank you to all my dedicated readers! More updates to come in a few days.
