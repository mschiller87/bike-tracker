I slept in today. Longer than I have all trip. I was treated to a fresh breakfast by the Flacks before hitting the road at 9am. Dave joined us for 15 miles and gave us the paparazzi treatment as we rode through downtown South Haven.

The first 35 miles were along the Kal-Haven trail, connecting South Haven to Kalamazoo via the old railroad tracks. It was nice, smooth, hard packed, crushed limestone. This was the last of the smooth riding for the day. The rest of the trails and roads rattled me to my core. My poor camera got the spin cycle in my front bag like nobody’s business. I stuffed my towel in around the camera in preparation for tomorrow’s ride to see if that improves the situation.

Along the way, we made a mandatory stop at Bells Brewing in Kalamazoo to welcome my Oberon “beer view mirror” to its homeland. The Bells merch store had everything I’ve ever wanted, but fortunately for my wallet, the space constraints on my bike limited me to a deck of cards and a pin.

The second brewery of the day, New Holland Brewing, was our choice for dinner in our final destination for the day; Battle Creek, MI. I hate to hate, but this place was kind of disappointing. The beers were detrimentally sweet, the food was not impressive, and the DJ out back was so bad we requested to move inside the restaurant. Despite this dinner strikeout, the vibes in Battle Creek were great. The evening temps were comfortable and live music in the park brought everyone downtown.

The late start resulted in the latest finish of the trip. We didn’t finish our ride until after 6pm. Hopefully this doesn’t haunt us tomorrow with less recovery time than normal.
