I guess that big day took its tole on me. I felt immediately fatigued when I started climbing up over Lolo pass this morning. After crawling to the top, I figured the hardest part of my day was over. Unfortunately, this was wrong. I faced a massive headwind all the way down the pass into Lolo and north into Missoula.

The extra effort was unaccounted for in my meal plan for the day and I bonked pretty hard. The snacks I was carrying weren’t cutting it. What I really needed was 2,000 hot calories straight to the face. That’s when old Ronald McDonald saved the day.

I crossed the board from Idaho into Montana! Woohoo third state here we come!

After getting into Missoula and showering off 4 days worth of life, I spent some time running errands that have been building up until I arrived in a city with bike shops and an REI. This town is really pretty and I like the layout. It feels like Michigan with mountains.

Tomorrow is going to be a zero day, figured after 2 weeks of nonstop biking it might be time to let my body rest a bit.

Go Knicks!
