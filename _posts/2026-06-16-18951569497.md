Another chill day out here in Montana. Woke up to a dry tent for the first time all trip, it’s starting to get pretty hot and dry.

Got my hair cut in Darby MT today. Feeling lighter and cooler with some extra airflow through the helmet

Stopped by a farmers market and picked up a bag of dried lavender to stuff in my nasty clothing bag. Mike bought a sweet knife. We split a half gallon of chocolate milk.

My newest strategy for staying fresh on this ride is stopping in at all the public libraries. They usually have a nice couch to lounge on, outlets to charge up, AC to cool down in, and a nice librarian who has most of the things you may need on the road.
