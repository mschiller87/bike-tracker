Biked around Missoula today running errands. Eating, buying gear, chilling in a library, eating again, reading, eating again.

I’ve included a few pictures of all the gear I have on this trip. My bike has the following storage areas: 

* 2x front fork bags
* 2x rear pannier bags (named Russell and  Angelina)
* Rear rack bag
* Handlebar bag
* 2x feed bags
* Frame bag
* Top post bag
* Saddle bag

Most of the gear is self explanatory. The different kits I brought I added text describing what they are (let me know if you would like deeper dives into any of the kits).

Some recent gear upgrades include: a larger pump, some extra replacement parts (brake pads, master link, valve stem), padded gloves, additional water bottle.

Doing this gear review was helpful as I came across items I haven’t touched yet and plan to send home. I don’t think my bags can get any smaller or lighter at this point in the trip. Once I clear the mountains I may send home some of my cold weather gear but that remains to be seen.

Missoula has been pretty awesome. I had the best donut of my life (huckleberry danish from Veera) and the neighborhoods have been beautiful to ride through.

Excited to see what Montana has in store for me!
