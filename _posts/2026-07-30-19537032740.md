I’ve spent the last 3 days chilling at home in Rochester. The weather has been fantastic, but I’ve been taking full advantage of the access to air conditioning and have been spending most of my time indoors. My dad and I watched The Odyssey in theaters, I revamped my bike setup after a trip to REI, and I ate some delicious food.

I really enjoyed seeing family and friends. It’s made it difficult to leave. I would be lying if the thought of throwing in the towel and ending the trip here hasn’t crossed my mind multiple times. Alas, after an early morning breakfast with friends, I find myself back in the saddle chipping away at the remaining miles.

Today’s ride was more of a warmup and a way get some miles out of the way for the coming days. I biked from my parent’s house to my brother’s apartment in Royal Oak. It was a much more enjoyable ride than I expected, with some protected bike paths and some beautiful homes along the way.

It’s always hard for me to leave my family. The time with them feels so fleeting and I’m not exactly sure when the next time I’ll see them is. I suppose that is one of the benefits of my move to New York, I’m just a short plane ride away.
