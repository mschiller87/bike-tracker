This morning I got to sleep in a bit. It was my first true hotel room of the whole trip and I was enjoying the white bed sheets and excessive amount of pillows.

I didn’t include it in my entry from yesterday, but last night after catching up with my Dad, we ran some errands then watched the US men’s team win their first knockout game inside a cozy Irish Pub. I helped him settle in some of his loose gear and tried to ease his nerves about the miles ahead. It made me think back to my first night, feeling completely overwhelmed thinking about the trip as a whole. Over time I’ve adjusted to thinking and planning a few days ahead and no further. One state at a time. Shrinking my trip into digestible chunks.

The ride today was mellow. No strong winds, no big hills. The hardest challenge to overcome today was the smell. Immediately upon leaving the Fort Collins city limits, we were smacked with some rancid aromas. First it was the smell of steaming cow poop from crowded dairy farms. Then it was hot garbage from the landfill. To top it all off, we finished with a long stretch of sour fertilizer steaming of the freshly sprayed earth. I miss the mountains. I’m afraid that the photography department is going to be lacking as compared to previous posts.

Services are pretty limited out here. We passed through one town and grabbed a slice of pizza. The town across from our campsite tonight has a small market that stocked less merchandise than a typical gas station. We spent the afternoon avoiding the sun and chugging water. A water bottle bath sufficed to rinse off the day’s sweat.

It was a good day. We’re gearing up for an early start tomorrow to tackle 60 miles.
