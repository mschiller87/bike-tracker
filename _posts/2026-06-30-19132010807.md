Like the boys from Boston said, I’m back in the saddle again. And boy do I have mixed feelings.

Wyoming was quite a challenge. It was beautiful, wild, windy, and for the most part, deserted. I found myself on all ends of the emotional spectrum in this state. I was thrilled to be crossing the border into colorful Colorado.

The Colorado welcoming was not what I hoped for. Instead of ice cream and downhills, I got demolished by a direct headwind and the gnarliest bumpy road of the trip. The last 27 miles of today’s ride just about rattled my bones apart. I’m figuratively throwing all of the resting and icing I did on my joints yesterday out the window. I didn’t really take many pictures today. Nothing caught my eye and I wasn’t really in the mood to extend my commute time on the already lengthy day.

My appetite has reached an all time high. I started the day off with 2 packets of oatmeal, 2 yogurts, and a package of raspberries. Over the course of the ride, I ate a fruit pie, 2 bags of chips, nuts, and a bar. Once I got to town, I stopped for a burger, then got 3 tacos at the food truck, then went back to the food truck for a quesadilla. I can promise you, I will wake up starving in the morning.

I’m relying on the fuel to carry me 100 miles to Fort Collins tomorrow where I will meet my dad! He is joining me on the leg from Fort Collins to Kansas City. I’m really excited to get to spend this time with him and show him the ropes of bike touring. Unfortunately, I think he chose the hottest, most boring section of the entire trail. Together we will get to explore the vast flatlands of eastern Colorado, Nebraska, and Kansas. While the terrain may not be very stimulating, at least we will have each other’s company.
