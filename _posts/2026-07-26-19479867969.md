Slept in again today. Not so long that we missed the hotel breakfast though. This may be a hot take, but I think hotel waffles are the best waffles. It’s hard to beat their consistently crispiness.

We hit the road around 8:30 this morning and biked past two prisons. The third prison came around mile 45. Despite the grim facilities just off the path, the trail conditions were spectacular and the surrounding forests were much more pleasant to look at. The route leap frogged across numerous trail systems. We moved at a much faster clip today due to the smooth paths and overcast conditions.

Later in the day a small trail-side booth caught our eye. They had a wide selection of home baked treats for sale and ice cold water. We indulged in a key lime pie and Carmel chocolate chunk cookie.

My good friend Dana met us at today’s brewery, Drafting Table. From there she biked with us the few more miles to her family’s house where we are spending the night. Thank you to the Pontious Family! We swam in Wolverine Lake right after finishing the ride then got Mexican food for dinner. A nice lake front sunset followed by a dead squirrel removal capped off the night before bed.

Tomorrow I will finish my traverse across Michigan in my hometown, Rochester Hills. I’ll be taking a few days off at home to chill out and prepare for the final leg to NYC!
