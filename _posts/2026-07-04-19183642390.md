This morning did not go according to plan. We slept in because of the low milage for the day. After getting dressed and beginning to pack our bikes before leaving, I discovered that my front tire was completely flat. There were 2 small punctures I must have gotten right at the end of the day yesterday. I threw my last spare tube in and patched the holy one. Since the next few days are going to be pretty remote, I backtracked to Walmart to grab an extra spare, only to find that they were all out of my tire size…

After finally getting rolling for the day, we were delighted to feel a tailwind. This carried us for most of the morning and made for a very easy first 40 miles.

The scenery today made me feel like I was biking along the board game of Catan. To my left was a golden field of wheat. To my right, an endless field of green corn stalks. Directly ahead of me stood the knight, a towering array of silos, visible from over five miles away, indicating the location of the next small town. 

If anyone can tell me why all the semis that passed me today left a sour smell in the air, that would be great.

My dad and I celebrated the holiday with a six pack of Montuckys, California burritos, and Kansas City hotdogs (in honor of Joey Chestnut).
