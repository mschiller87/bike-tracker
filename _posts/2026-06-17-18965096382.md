Started the day climbing over Saint Joseph pass. 2,200 feet of vertical climbing to reach my highest point yet, 7,264 feet.

Crossed over the Continental Divide and got to see one of the CDT markers! That means every drop of rain from here on out leads back to the Atlantic Ocean.

Got lunch in Wisdom MT, picked up a couple beers, then made the push to our final destination. This area is called the Big Hole because of the circular shaped mountains that surround the area. It’s full of agriculture that uses flood irrigation to water the land. This environment provides the perfect breeding ground for the largest, nastiest, most blood thirsty mosquitos I’ve ever come across. These monstrosities were able to keep up with me while pedaling at 19 mph and lay siege to my highly sugar enriched blood. It became obvious to me that sleeping outside tonight was not an option.

Luckily, the Bunkhouse in Jackson was the answer to my prayers. This building serves as the post office, laundry mat, bar, and hostel for the town of 36 people. The top floor of this 116 year old building is full of queen beds for rent, and comes complete with couches, satellite tv, and a warm shower. I’m in heaven.

Today I crossed the 1,000 mile mark! In my head, I thought this moment would feel more significant, but really, it just felt like another day of biking. I can feel myself slowly starting to lean into the adventure side of this trip rather than the mission side. It’s very easy for me to focus on the task that needs completed and loose site of the joy of the process. After 17 days, and over 1000 miles, it’s become clear that this trip is going to last a while and that I may as well enjoy every moment.
