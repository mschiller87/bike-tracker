A bad weather day was overdue. I battled the cold, the rain, and the wind all day today.

I awoke to a slight drizzle in the Virginia City KOA. Right after packing up camp and hitting the road, the drizzle turned into steady rain. Once I reached the top of Virginia Pass, the steady rain turned into a downpour. My wet legs started to freeze up with the cold wind blowing over them, I had to pull over and put on my rain pants. It was at this point that the voices in my head convinced me that 15 miles  was enough for the day and I should find the closest motel to hide in.

Energy levels were at an all time low after stopping at the grocery store in Ennis MT. I was feeling annoyed with the weather and didn’t want to talk with anyone. I resorted to putting my head down, plugging in some headphones, and grinding out the rest of the daily milage.

We were able to find a cheap cabin to rent for the night to avoid camping in the storm. Some warm chicken noddle soup, alfredo pasta, and pop tarts for dinner cured all my ailments and set me up for a deep and rejuvenating sleep.

Tomorrow we enter Yellowstone National Park!!!
