I didn’t sleep that well last night, despite being indoors and warm. I think the altitude may be playing a role.

Huge milestone achievement today: I successfully located and consumed my first roller dog of the trip🌭. Seeing the big goose egg on my stats page for the past 18 days has haunted me, but no longer. Bring on the glizzies, unleash the pork pistols, load the grease missiles, it’s dog time.

The biking today was pretty uneventful besides the aforementioned encounter, until I arrived to my final destination. The towns of Nevada City and Virginia City are beautifully preserved towns from the 1800’s when they were gold mining towns and the original Montana State Capital. It was a combination of really interesting history and architecture, and really expensive tourism and dinning. The bison ravioli was good but not quite worth the price tag. 

We’ve started to see a lot more bike tourists heading west. These guys started their journeys back in April and are nearing completion. At this point, I would describe cross-country bike touring as an active retirement organization. I can count the number of people I’ve met under the age of 55 on 4 fingers.
