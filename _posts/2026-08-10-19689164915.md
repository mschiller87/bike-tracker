This morning was the coldest morning since I left the mountains back in Colorado with temperatures in the high 50’s. So cold, that for the first time, I wish I had my sleeping bag instead of my thin sheet I’ve been sleeping under. For the first few hours of biking I threw on my windbreaker safety vest to keep my chest warm. A hot bacon egg and cheese and cinnamon roll for breakfast also added some warmth.

I biked the final flat miles of the Erie Canal Trail before the Mohawk River runs into the Hudson and the path turns south towards Manhattan. The trails today were gorgeous and the reintroduction of hills added a bit more variety to the landscape. As we neared the larger cities, the bike paths came alive with people and pets.

Albany was the big target for the day. Mike and I arrived around noon, just in time for a showing of the new spider man movie. We figured if the movie was lousy, it was worth the money for a nap in a comfortable air conditioned chair. After the movie, we took a lap around downtown to see the State Capitol building and some of the surrounding landmarks. The severity of the hills in Albany caught us off guard. With steep city roads reminiscent of San Francisco, we left town out of breath and dripping in sweat. Luckily our hotel for the night was only 6 miles further and had some cold showers waiting for us.

One of Mike’s friends drove out and treated us to a pizza and wing dinner. I followed up dinner with my first Stewart’s ice cream, which was a delicious success.

This is my last night with my friend Mike. Our paths deviate tomorrow morning as he heads toward home in Boston. I’m so grateful to have run into this guy way back in Oregon. I never would have made it this far without his friendship and company. Tomorrow we will say our goodbyes after our last 10 shared miles.

T-2 days until I reach NYC!!!!
