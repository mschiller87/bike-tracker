Super early start to the day today, rolling at 5:30am. Even this early in the morning, the humidity was high and it was almost 70 degrees. It only went up from there and without a lick of shade for the first 70 miles, this turned into the sweatiest ride of my life.

Starting before the sun provided some beautiful sunrise lighting. The light mixing with some morning fog made it feel like flames rising up from the valleys in the fields.

At this point, I think I’ve had Casey’s breakfast pizza for at least five days in a row. I’m not sure if this is something to be concerned about or something to celebrate. There is a Casey’s in almost every town and they are consistently clean, air conditioned, and always has an ice machine to refill bottles. The same cannot be said for any other gas station chain. I’m afraid for the day to come when I go inside my last Casey’s of the trip. I probably won’t know it in the moment, but all of the sudden, it’ll be gone…

The day was too crazy on paper, but the heat started to take its tole on my around mile 60. My water ran low, and all that remained was boiling hot. I took a break under a lone tree on the side of the road. Without the constant airflow from biking, the sweat streams running down my body multiplied, attracting a plethora of flying insects. Needless to say, it was quite difficult to relax.

The highlight of the day was finally linking up with the Katy Trail, the longest rail-trail in the US. The trail was a nice crushed gravel path with the first consistent shade of the day. This took us to our final destination at the casino in Boonville, MO. I’m not the biggest fan of staying in hotels because it doesn’t feel like I get to see or experience much of the town, but I would have paid anything for AC and a cold shower at the time of check in.🌭
