My dues have been paid, I finally got some tailwind!

The first 20 miles of today’s ride were straight east through Sinclair, Wyoming, home of a massive Sinclair oil refinery (the dinosaur gas brand). Most of this section was on interstate 80. Under normal circumstances, this would have sucked. However, with a 25mph tailwind, brand new pavement, and minimal traffic during church hours, it was incredible. I was smiling ear to ear as I blasted down the shoulder in my highest gear. I promised myself, I will never end a day early when there are active tailwinds, I’d rather bike into the night than give up this precious opportunity.

Sadly, all good things must come to an end. My route turned south and the trail winds turned to cross winds. Nothing to cry about compared to previous days. The landscape was incredibly bland, the most interesting feature being the fact that I could see cloud shadows moving across the desert floor before they engulfed me in darkness.

I arrived in Saratoga before noon, one of the shortest and quickest rides of the whole trip. I’m staying at a local church that hosts bikers in their annex house. It’s basically a free Airbnb complete with my own bedroom, a full kitchen, and a bike shop garage. Saratoga is also home to Hobo Hot Springs, a free public natural hot springs. This what my body has been dreaming of since I started biking every day.

I grilled some chicken for dinner and was joined by a young 6 point buck. He seemed pretty familiar with the joint and had no issue with the space I was occupying, he also didn’t seem too interested in chicken. He just laid down, watched me cook, scratched his head, took a piss, then headed back to work.

In other news, the roller stations are becoming bountiful, I think I’m finally entering the promise land. Today I snagged a breakfast tornado and a jalapeño cheddar dog. Adding +2 to the stats page.🌭🌭
