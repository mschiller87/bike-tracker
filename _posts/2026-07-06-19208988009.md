Smoking hot day today. Headwinds helped keep the temperature manageable for the first few hours but resulted in a battle for every mile. After 12 pm it turned ugly. No amount of hydration seemed to be enough. Shade was few and far between, we jumped on every opportunity we came across.

Earlier in the day, I was more appreciative of our surroundings. This part of Nebraska is very green and luscious with rolling hills and soft grass. Our destination for the day, McCook Nebraska, is apparently known as tree city. I have yet to see a good reason for this. At my first stop for the day, I grabbed two breakfast tornados, I’m counting these as roller dogs 🌭🌭(sue me).

Walmart was our first stop in town for some cold drinks, pop-tart reload, and some AC. Then we made our way over to the YMCA for the pool, showers, and more AC. We weren’t having any luck calling local churches for a place to stay for the night. Luckily, we met Eric in the pool who offered us a spot at his mom’s business. She runs a used book store/yoga studio/event space in downtown McCook. It was the perfect space to roll out our pads and protect us from the hot hellscape outside.

We watched USA fumble the bag at the local axe throwing bar before calling it an early night. Planning to get rolling as early as possible tomorrow to avoid the afternoon heat. 

Pop-tart review: inspired by @cloe, I’ve decided to share some thoughts on the newest release in food tech, the Super Stuffed Pop-Tarts. These come in single sleeved pouches with 5/box, unlike the double pouches with 4/box in the typical variety. From previous analyses completed before through hiking the John Muir Trail, I found that Pop-Tarts pack the most calories/gram out of all other typical snack foods (more than nuts and dried fruit!). I believe these super stuffed pop-tarts take them to the next level. These are like double stuffed Oreos, more of what you like, less of what you don’t like. So far I’ve tried the lava cake and cinnamon caramel flavor. I just restocked with the strawberry and will let you know what I think. Give them a try if you want to get out of the rock you’re living under.

Molten Lava Cake SS - 8.5/10
Cinnamon Caramel SS- 7/10
Strawberry SS- TBD
