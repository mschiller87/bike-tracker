Well, I have good news and bad news. Good news, it wasn’t as windy as yesterday. Bad news, only slightly.

There was one gas station along the route today, other than that, it was completely barren desert. Any green grass had been munched up by the cows, only sage brush remained. I ate a burrito and a berry danish at the gas station, then pushed on. 

The morning was fairly calm with a slight cross wind. Right when I was starting to think I got lucky and avoided the wind for the day, I was struck by my first gust. These gusts continued for the last 30 miles and into the evening. They persisted even in downtown Rawlins while I grocery shopped and picked up a Dominos pizza, it makes everything harder and louder.

I saw some cool wildlife over the past 24 hours. I spotted a few Pronghorns, the fastest land mammal in North America reaching speeds of over 55mph. I watched a Western Meadowlark sing his tune. I dodged a Prairie Rattlesnake shakin his tail. 
The wind has completely worn me down at this point. I am planning to take a rest day in the next town to let my joints heal and my legs relax. There is a church hostel and a hot springs in town so it seems as good a place as any to spend an extra day.
