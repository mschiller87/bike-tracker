An early morning start got us rolling right after some rain dissipated. Temperatures were cool and the sun was hidden behind overcast clouds. It was the most consistent comfortable weather I’ve had since leaving the mountains.

The storm clouds above cast dramatic lighting across the farm land. There’s something so satisfying about the perfect order in modern day crop fields. The corduroy like texture, clean edges, and contours scratch both the mathematical and artistic sides of my brain. I tried my best to capture these views with my camera, but nothing came out as impressive as it looked in person, so you’ll have to trust me on this one.

The morning’s storm blew east in the same direction we were traveling. The tail end of the bulbous storm clouds left rays of light peeking through the thick morning air. Unfortunately, we eventually caught up to the storm and proceeded to get soaked to the bone for the last 10 miles. An abandoned gas station awning provided some protection from the rain just before arriving to Washington, Kansas.

Today was a big day for Casey’s (my favorite place ever). We had one at mile 30 and one in our final destination. For breakfast, I got the classics: a loaded burrito and a slice of breakfast pizza. For lunch, I tried the BBQ bacon cheeseburger sliders and a spicy crispy chicken sandwich. Later in the evening, I returned to get some change for the laundry mat. My final visit was to get some cold drinks. In total, I visited the Washington Casey’s three times today, wearing three different shirts (because of the laundry), and checked out with the same cashier all three times. I don’t think she recognized me.
