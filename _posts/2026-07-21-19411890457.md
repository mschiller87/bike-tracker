The bike trails in Illinois are phenomenal. However, the roads are horrendous. I’d say less than 25% of my route through the state has been on bike trails, which means the other 75% of the time my small boy butt bones are getting thunder clapped by each crack in the road. Thankfully I have a healthy relationship with my saddle, the Brooks B-70. She’s rockhard, but after hundreds of hours together cheek to cheek, she’s molded to my exact dimensions. Instead of each crack in the road sending shockwaves straight up my tailbone, the impact is spread around my greater butt region. I still feel pretty damaged at the end of each day, but I imagine it could be way worse. Thanks Brooksie.

I was moving with purpose today, not for any real reason, it just felt good to fly. I had to slow myself down when I realized I was going to pass by the restaurant I wanted to get lunch at before they even opened. That spot was Polk-A-Dot Drive In. Opened in the 1950s, it’s a well kept dinner serving all my favorite things. I got a double bacon cheese burger with French fries and a vanilla malt, all for less than $10. It tasted like what I imagine McDonald’s tasted like before they opened their first corporate office.

Route 66 is full of strange road-side attractions. Each town seems to have a collection of large figurines and any other creative ideas the other towns haven’t thought of yet. Today, I stopped by the Braidwood Zoo, a small 10’x10’ square plot of fenced in land containing six very strange manmade animals. Each was constructed with slightly different methods, but all contained creepy beady eyes. I also got a picture with a jump sized Elvis and the Gemini Giant.

Finally, the weather seems to be cooling down a bit. Headwinds kept me a comfortable temperature all morning. The winds picked up to a level beyond just free AC in the afternoon and started to get on my nerves. Luckily I didn’t have far to go to get to my hotel in Wilmington, IL. It’s my last night in Illinois and I can’t say I’m going to miss it. At this point in the trip, I’m very eager to get to where I’m going and spend my days doing other things besides just biking.
