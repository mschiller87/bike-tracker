I slept so hard last night that I woke up sore. I spent so much of the night deep in dreamland, that my body didn’t feel like it relaxed at all. After my 5am alarm, I shook the drowsiness off with a pint of chocolate milk and stretched out my achy muscles by rolling around in bed. 

We had a little bit of fog again this morning. Not enough to risk visibility issues but enough to cast some beautiful sunrise light. I turned around at one point to capture a video of the light passing through the branches of a tree sending beams of misty orange sunshine outwards like fingers.

Kansas has not been as flat as I was led to believe. The north eastern corner that we biked through was carved out by glaciers, resulting in lots of rolling hills. It’s nothing like climbing the mountains out west, but it leads to a never ending changing of gears and speeds going up and down all day. Today is my last day in Kansas. We are staying in Atchison, Kansas, right along the Missouri River separating it from its namesake state.

The city pool was the largest of the trip and was the least expensive at $1/person. It was a welcomed reprieve from the heat and a good finish line for the 67 mile day.
