We crossed the Missouri River first thing in the morning. I was welcomed to the new state with immediate hills and dense forests. This was the starkest transition across any state-line so far. Back in Kansas, some local guy got pretty defensive after we called Kansas flat. After just one day in Missouri, climbing hills over 14% grade, I’m starting to doubt that man has ever left his hometown. For those of you who have no frame of reference for what a 14% climb looks like, all I can do to help illustrate this is say that the steepest grade I’ve climbed this far into the trip has been 8%… These steep Missouri grades require me to stand up to pedal, using my full body weight, in my lowest gear, to move fast enough to maintain my balance.

Besides the challenges noted above, it was a pretty short day. We arrived in Smithville, MO around 1 pm and checked in to the Smithville Historic Inn. This place is a museum on the ground floor, with bedrooms on the second floor. Museum feels like a stretch, it’s more like a really old house that the hosts have packed with all of their flea market finds. Nevertheless, it’s a unique place to spend the night, and a cool town to explore.

For dinner I ordered a burger, big surprise, and it turned out to be the best of the trip. The restaurant we went to uses a special mixture of meat for their patties that includes local beef, bacon, and brisket. The burger had strips of bacon and bacon jam on top. I think it should have been called “Bacon Three Ways”, but instead they named it after the restaurant, “Gizmos Burger”.

Today was my last day with my Dad, he flys out of Kansas City tomorrow. It was great having his company and I was super impressed with his strength and durability out here. He managed to join for the flattest section of all, and is getting out of here right when the hills start back up (by his design). This next section may be a bit lonely as I embark on a solo stretch from Missouri to Michigan. It brings me back to feelings I had at the beginning of the trip. However, this time should be different. I know what I’m doing, I have more friends and family waiting to join me, and I’m in too deep to back out now.
