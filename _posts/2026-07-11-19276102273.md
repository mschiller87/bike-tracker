Suuuuuuper thick fog this morning. Thickness levels were borderline dangerous. Viability was probably no more than 30 yards. A police officer pulled us over and asked if he could give us a ride anywhere because he was worried about the lack of visibility. Luckily, the fog burned off shortly after our conversation with him and we felt much more comfortable on the road.

We followed route 36 all day. The landscapes and lighting were gorgeous. Rolling hills of green and golden corn, oak trees, wind turbines, and babbling creeks. The biggest thing missing from the ride today was shade. Shade went missing somewhere back in the mountains of Idaho. It’s only since Colorado that I’ve really started missing it as temperatures have consistently climbed over 85F. I’m hopeful that as my route starts to incorporate more trails, shade will find its way back to me.

I’m afraid I may have hit my Casey’s limit. Today I got free french fries and pizza for signing up for their rewards program, but it didn’t really hit the spot. To counter this lack of enthusiasm for fried food, I tackled some pineapple and blackberries for lunch, then a banana and grapes for a snack. Hopefully I wake up tomorrow feeling whole again and ready to fry.

The pool in Seneca, our destination for the night, was the best of the trip so far. It featured the typical diving boards, a large lap pool, an obstacle course, and two water slides. My riding partner, Mike, enjoyed the water slide so much we may have ridden it over 20 times. It reminded me of the zip line bit in Time Robinson’s show, “I Think You Should Leave”.

Mexican for dinner was righteous, FIFA soccer games were entertaining (Go England!), motel AC is cold, and I’m not sunburned. Can’t complain.
