Today’s ride crossed through the Wind River Reservation. It was a 70 mile stretch of completely barren desert wilderness outside of 2 small villages single gas stations. The threat of afternoon storms combined with the lack of services kept me moving at a fast clip all day.

Similar to all the gas stations I’ve visited already, the 2 on today’s route did not delivery any roller meats. I settled for a microwaved burrito at the first stop and a microwaved pizza at the second.

Shortly after leaving the last gas station for the day, the storm clouds that I had been trying to outrun finally caught up to me. The sun was almost completely blocked out, creating an eerie twilight world at 12pm. The winds picked up and thunder rolled around me. The skies opened up and soaked through my rain gear in a matter of minutes. Lightning struck within a few miles of me, assuming my memory that sound travels at 1 mile/second is correct. I arrived in Lander WY to the local bike shop looking a little worse for wear. I was hoping to get some help re-sealing my rear tubeless tire that was punctured in Yellowstone. While trying to find the small hole that caused that deflation, we found a nail poking straight through the tire that must have been picked up at some point today. I ended up replacing the whole tire with the bombproof marathon plus tires commonly used by cross country tourists.

The camping location for tonight is at Lander’s City Park. It also happens to be the first of their summer concert series tonight with a free show for the whole town. It feels like I’m at a music festival with food trucks and live music right outside my zipper door. Might be a late night for me.
