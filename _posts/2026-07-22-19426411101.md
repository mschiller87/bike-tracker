I crossed over the 3000 mile mark today! If I would have chosen to cross the US along the southern tier, I would be across by now! Luckily, I chose a very long, much less direct route, and I still have over 1000 miles left!

Today’s ride was the first half of my connection from Route 66 to the Great Lake to Lake trail in Michigan. I routed along as many bike trails as I could. The Wauponsee Glacier Trail brought me to Manhattan, Old Plank Road Trail took me through Frankfort, Thorn Creek Trail wound through Chicago Heights and Glenwood, and the Prairie Dune Trail took me the last 15 miles to Indiana Dunes. 

For my single day in Indiana, my experience with the drivers here was not great. It could be that I’m just in more populated areas or that I’m used to the kindness of drivers in California, but today I had more close calls with cars than the rest of the trip combined. Drivers didn’t yield to me at crosswalks and tried to pass me on roads that didn’t have enough space. Up until today, I’ve been so pleased with caution and care exhibited by all the drivers who have crossed paths with me. This just means I’ll have to up my safety game and car avoidance.

Today was the first day since the mountains that I wasn’t dripping in sweat. Dare I say, early in the morning it was actually chilly. Once I got to my final destination, the sun came out right on time to spend few hours on the beach at Indiana Dunes National Park. It was a lovely day for reading but not for swimming. The red flag was up and lifeguards were not letting people swim due to the high winds and surf. Waves were coming in at 5-8’ high. I’m hoping the conditions are better tomorrow so I get at least one swim in Lake Michigan before I leave.
