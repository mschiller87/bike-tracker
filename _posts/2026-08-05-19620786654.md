Early start for a big day. There was a Sheetz right down the street from my campsite so I had the golden opportunity to sample some of their food. I stand by my previous assessment, this food is made for hungover people, or people on very long road trips. I got cinnamon french toast sticks with Cinnabon dip and a breakfast burrito.

The morning’s scenery consisted of lake views between people’s houses and rolling vineyards. When visible, the coast line of Erie transitioned from shallow beaches to dramatic bluffs the further east I went. The weather has been fantastic lately. Anything under 80 degrees feels like heaven with the wind blowing.

My body felt really good all day. It fills me with energy when I think about it. I feel so grateful to get to experience this trip with a strong healthy body and it makes me want to pedal faster and further. I crossed my last state line today when I entered New York. Strangely enough, NY will be my longest state of all with close to 550 miles of biking.

I discovered a small ferry that shleps people and bikes across the Buffalo River. I thought it would be a cool extension of the ride to bike down the harbor state park then ride the ferry to the main land. I ended up waiting in the sun for the ferry for over 45 minutes. It turns out the ferry captain recently had his identify stolen and was busy on the phone with his credit union on the other side of the river. I regretted taking that extension.

Once I got into Buffalo, I met up with my friend Sarah. We haven’t seen each other in over 3 years but it felt like we picked up right where we left off. She took me to a bar that is known for their Buffalo wings and I attempted to blend in with the locals by getting it with blue cheese and a Labatt Blue. Thank you for hosting me Sarah! Sorry I go to bed and get up so early!
