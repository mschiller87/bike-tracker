Call me lame, but I had absolutely no desire to bike any further to round out a satisfying 100 miler. I got to my destination and I got off my bike, end of riding. The sweat, humidity, and miles are doing bad things to my butt. My saddle did a great job for so long, but I’m crumbling under the conditions in New York. If my butt didn’t hurt, I think I could go much further.

Today’s ride followed the Erie Canal trail again. Some small sections required biking on the road but most of the day was on dedicated bike paths. I biked over one of the canal’s aqueducts. These massive concrete structures allow the canal to pass over another river without disrupting any of the flow of either water way. I also got to follow a couple canal boats heading east. They create a mesmerizing set of waves in the glassy still water as they chug past.

I got a donut and a burrito for breakfast. Nothing has satisfied my craving Mexican craving since leaving California, I should stop getting my hopes up. The real star of the show was my lunch stop where I got my first New York style pizza. I got a slice of chicken bacon ranch, and hot honey pepperoni with ricotta dollops. The pizza was so good it distracted me from my sore butt for a few miles.

Within 10 miles of my final destination, Syracuse NY, I came across a really comfy lawn chair set out with a sign that said “rest stop”. Shortly after sitting down with a loud old man sigh, I got an alert from my phone that a storm was quickly approaching. I checked my map and realized if I left immediately I could beat it before it hit Syracuse. I said goodbye to my short lived comfort and started racing the storm east. The adrenaline did an even better job of keeping my attention away from my butt. I blasted past the New York Fair Grounds, and Lake Onondaga, before getting my bones and bolts rattled on some mangled city roads through downtown Syracuse.

I am staying with Keri, a Warmshowers host, in her beautiful apartment downtown Syracuse. She has an incredible balcony overlooking the city, and a pool and hot tub that I got some good use out of. Her AC works really well and the guest bed is super comfy. I’m turning off my alarm for tomorrow and making the most of this. Warmshowers can be such a crapshoot sometimes and you never know what you’re going to get, but tonight, I won the lottery.

Despite me saying that the star of the show was my pizza lunch, I have returned to this entry after eating dinner to tell you the real real star of the show was my rigatoni carbonara from Abbiocco. They served it with warm focaccia and a homemade garlic, parmesan, honey butter. My god it was delicious. I’m ending the day with a full belly in a cozy bed with time to read my forgotten book. Life is good on the road today!
