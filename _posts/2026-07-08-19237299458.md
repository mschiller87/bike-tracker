I’ve gotten some feedback that my journal entries focus too heavily on food. It made me reflect on my trip as a whole and I realized something. I spend virtually the entire day doing one of three things: Eating, Sleeping, and Biking. If I’m not doing one of those three things, I’m either planning to do them, hoping to do them, or trying to avoid them.

Today’s ride was pretty short, but I made the mistake of thinking it would be easy. The heat blasted me like a gut punch with 6 miles to go. A lack of electrolytes mixed with a salivating craving for a Casey’s Bacon Cheeseburger pizza made my knees weak and my vision blurry. I was rewarded for my determination immediately upon entering Franklin, NE with the AC from the open-air beer refrigerators and the freshly made pizza of my dream’s.

After a sweaty shirtless laundromat run, another public pool finally brought our core temperatures back to steady state levels. We lounged here for a few hours straddling the peak heat of the day at 95F.

Our accommodations for the night were with a local couple we found on WarmShowers. This website is specifically designed for touring cyclists to find housing and support during their trips. Most of the hosts are bikers themselves and love nothing more than to help out a fellow cyclists. This is my first time officially using this site and was very pleased with our hosts Mike and Ann. They cooled down the RV in their yard for us to sleep in and welcomed us in their home for showers and dinner. The people of Nebraska continue to set the hospitality bar higher.

We learned that the slogan for Nebraska used to be “honestly, it’s not for everyone” and I thought that was honest and clever.
