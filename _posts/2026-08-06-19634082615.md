Sneaky early exit so as to not wake Sarah up. The first 25 miles seemed to fly by, despite the fact that I had to double back after finding that the trail was closed 5 miles into the ride. A combination of frustration about the wasted milage and disappointment in the lack of signage may have contributed to the speedy quarter century.

I stoped at Tom’s Diner for breakfast and got a waffle and bacon. At this point, breakfast food has turned into more of a vessel for me to consume as much syrup as possible. Savory breakfast sits heavy in my stomach, but sweet breakfast turns to jet fuel. With the waffle as my fuel and the bacon as my electrolytes (don’t fact check this), I was ready to hit the road.

By road I really mean trail. Today’s ride was almost exclusively on the car-less Erie Canal trail. A nearly perfectly flat path except for some steep downhills at the lochs. The silence I discovered on the trail was beautiful. Besides the crunching gravel beneath my tires and the bustle at each canal town, the trail followed the almost motionless canal in total silence. It was peaceful. I am trying to savor every moment as I draw ever closer to what may be the loudest living environment I have ever experienced.

Miles 25-75 were some of the best ever. I felt invincible and was already starting to think about how much further I was willing to go beyond my planned destination for the day. In my head, I could have done 150+ miles today. That all changed after lunch. I ate a combo chicken and beef shawarma wrap and sat for too long. Leaving the restaurant I felt like dead weight. Everything ached and I felt fatigued and lightheaded. Chugging some water helped with the dizziness but the fatigue dragged on all the way to my final destination in Rochester, NY.

Once in town, I quickly resupplied with some chocolate milk, power-aid, and a donut. I enjoyed my sugar meal on a shaded adirondack chair overlooking the Genesee River. I met my Warmshowers host for the night then headed to dinner. No amount of cold showers could fight the heat and humidity that persisted into the evening. I am writing this entry shirtless in bed in a hot house with no fans or AC. If only I could carry with me some of the cold air that I have been so spoiled with recently. My body is in such desperate need of sleep that not even this heat will keep me from it. I’m done with all my tasks and it’s 8pm, goodnight New York.
