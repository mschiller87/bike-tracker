I strongly believe that fatigue is mostly mental. Today should have been a breeze being less than half my daily milage for the past few days. However, mentally I was prepared for 48 miles, so by the end of the day, I felt the same as I did on my longer rides. I’m sure the drop in milage is great for muscle recovery in the long run, but today I’m still exhausted.

Mike and I ducked out of the marina quick this morning to avoid any more run-ins with the dock-master. With the small day ahead of us, we took every opportunity to sightsee and linger in establishments. I investigated my first canal lock, we met a lively couple from Arizona who have toured all over the world, and we lost track of time in two separate Stewart’s Shops. I’ve decided that Stewart’s might actually be better than Casey’s for the following reasons: they have indoor seating, and their milk and ice cream are delicious. Casey’s beats Stewart’s in every other category.

We burned a bunch of time in Amsterdam taking advantage of some shade and the proximity of local restaurants. Mike made the controversial decision to eat gas station chili for lunch, and I got to enjoy the airborne aftermath for the rest of the day. For dinner, I tried some award winning chicken wings. A unique blend of buffalo and parmesan garlic on some expertly crisped wings with some homemade blue cheese. 

After dinner, we biked the last 5 miles of the day in some incredibly comfortable evening conditions. Overall, the weather today was superb. I accredit it to lower humidity. So much lower, that going to bed shower-less doesn’t feel like I’m a piece of steaming garbage. Tonight will be my last time pitching my tent and camping on this trip! We are camped out at Lock 10 under some trees serenaded by the sound of rushing water, the highway, and the occasional train.

I only have three (3) days of biking left before reaching Manhattan and seeing my love (Clarabel). Then just one more day of biking before the true Transcontinental journey is complete and I dunk my front tire in the Atlantic Ocean!
